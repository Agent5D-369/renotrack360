# Payment and invoice transitions

Payment creation previously incremented invoice paid/balance regardless of status, while editing did not reconcile either invoice. The shared payment command now counts only COMPLETED receipts and atomically reconciles both invoices when a payment moves. Full refunds exclude the original completed amount; partial refunds require a separately reviewed adjustment and are not simulated by shrinking the receipt. This records bookkeeping only and never calls a payment provider.

A serialized Flipside finance transaction coordinates invoice edits, concurrent receipts and moves. Decimal values, current membership and all present parent owners are checked. A unique request plus retained input digest makes repeats safe; updates require the exact viewed timestamp. Duplicate Stripe references are refused. Before/after payment and invoice snapshots are immutable PaymentRevision records; revision and audit commit with the money change. Audit failure rolls everything back.

Invoice forms derive paid from completed receipts and cannot accept an independent paid total. Subtotal plus tax must equal total. Stale invoice edits fail. Invoices with receipt records cannot be reassigned to a different job/client. Existing invoice discrepancies stop mutations until evidence is reconciled; they are never silently erased.

`/payments/reconciliation` is read-only and distinguishes recorded invoice/job totals from linked completed receipts. The production scan before this change found all 3 invoices consistent, but all 3 job-level paid totals differing from receipt records. Those historical values remain untouched. The job page now shows a warning when its recorded paid total differs. No guess is made about missing receipts versus incorrect historical totals. Explicit job reconciliation, deposit schedules and contractual change accounting remain WU007B; this release does not certify the entire financial workflow.

The existing payment screens retain their fields with a server-rendered form, cent precision, safe default PENDING and an explanation that recording a status is not a provider transaction. The retained history is visible on payment detail. Tests cover transitions, cents, concurrency, retries, stale edits, moves, full refunds, invalid/foreign/revoked input, provider duplication, rollback, immutable history, derived invoice totals and discrepancy preservation. No live payment is used as a test fixture.
